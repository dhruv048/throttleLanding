Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Throttle collects on this website, why, and what you can do about it. Contact us at blackstellarholdings@gmail.com with any privacy question or request.

1. What we collect

  • When you pre-register: your email address, the username you choose and, if you give it, your bike.
  • Payment details: handled by Paddle, our reseller. Your card details go straight to Paddle; we never see or store them. Paddle tells us the payment status, amount, currency and its customer and transaction IDs.
  • Where you came from: campaign tags in the link you followed (such as utm_source) and the referring website, so we know which posts bring riders in.
  • How the site is used: page views and form steps (for example, opening checkout), linked to a random visitor ID stored in your browser. We also use Google Analytics, which collects usage data such as pages visited, device and approximate location.

2. Why we use it

  • To process your pre-registration, reserve your username and attach Throttle Pro to your app account (to perform our agreement with you).
  • To email you when Throttle launches and about your pre-registration.
  • To understand and improve the website and our marketing (our legitimate interest).
  • To prevent fraud and meet legal and tax obligations.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't send marketing emails beyond launch news without your consent.

3. Who we share it with

  • Paddle, which processes payments as Merchant of Record under its own privacy policy.
  • Supabase, which hosts our database.
  • Vercel, which hosts the website.
  • Google, for Google Analytics.

These providers may store data outside your country. We only share what each one needs to provide its service. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Cookies and browser storage

Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits. The site also stores a few small values in your browser: your light or dark theme choice, a random visitor ID, and the campaign that brought you here. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings, or block analytics cookies with your browser or an extension.

5. How long we keep it

We keep pre-registration records for as long as needed to provide Throttle Pro and to meet tax and accounting rules. Usage data is kept for up to 26 months. If you ask us to delete your data, we'll do so unless we have to keep it by law.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or object to how we use it. Email blackstellarholdings@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also be able to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Throttle is not meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
