Billing questions

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Recording rides with live stats, offline and screen-off, stays free with no time limit and no card needed.

What does the $9.99 pre-registration include?

Early access to the app, your reserved username, the Founding Rider badge and a founding discount on Throttle Pro. See our terms.

How is Throttle Pro billed after launch?

$49.99 a year, renewing yearly until you cancel. You can cancel at any time and keep Pro until the end of the year you paid for. Your rides and data stay on the Free plan.

Who handles payment and tax?

Our reseller and Merchant of Record, Paddle, handles payment, invoices and sales tax or VAT. Prices are in US dollars and tax is added at checkout depending on where you live.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, a full refund within 14 days of paying. See our refund policy.
