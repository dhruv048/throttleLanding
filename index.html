FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Throttle?

A ride tracker and social app made for motorcycles. It records your route, distance, time, average and top speed with GPS, makes a shareable ride card from every ride, keeps per-bike kilometres in your garage, and lets you follow other riders.

What do I get for $1.99?

Throttle Pro, ready on your account the day the app launches. Pre-registering costs $1.99, once; after launch, Pro costs $15.99 a year.

How do I claim it?

Sign in to the Throttle app with the same email you used here. Your pre-registration is attached to your account automatically.

When does the app launch?

We're in final testing on iOS and Android. Founding riders hear first, by email, the day it's out.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Reply to your Stripe receipt and we'll refund you, no questions asked.

Is my payment safe?

Payment happens on Stripe's secure checkout page. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our servers.
