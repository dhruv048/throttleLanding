Refund Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

We want every founding rider to be happy with their pre-registration. If you change your mind, here's how refunds work.

14-day refund

You can get a full refund within 14 days of paying for your pre-registration. You don't need to give a reason.

How to ask for one

  • Email blackstellarholdings@gmail.com from the address you pre-registered with, or
  • use the link in your Paddle receipt email, or visit paddle.net to find your order.

Paddle, our reseller and Merchant of Record, processes the refund back to your original payment method. Refunds usually show up within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

What happens after a refund

Your pre-registration is cancelled: you won't get Throttle Pro at launch through it, and any username you reserved is released for others to take.

If Throttle Pro doesn't launch

If we decide not to launch Throttle Pro, we'll refund every pre-registration in full, whenever you paid.

Questions

Email blackstellarholdings@gmail.com. These rules sit alongside our terms and any rights you have under consumer law.
